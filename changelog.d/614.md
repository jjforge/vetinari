section: New features
- [user] `vetinari replan [--dry-run]` re-plans the waves of a running, parked or stopped campaign that have not started yet from the current config and tracker — a fixed `Touches:` line, a better `fileSet`, a new `waivedFiles` entry — without stopping and relaunching (the log is not archived). Started waves keep their numbers and the new waves follow them; the loop runs them from its next wave boundary, or a stopped campaign from its next `redrive`. It never adds or removes an issue, refuses whole (logging nothing) when an unstarted issue has no confident file set or an open blocker outside the campaign, prints the current and new remaining waves and every issue that moved, and logs nothing when the plan is unchanged (#614).
- [api] A new `replan { waves, was? }` event in `orchestrator.jsonl`: the reducer keeps every started wave at its index and replaces the unstarted tail with `waves`; the dashboard, status line and feed show the re-planned waves (`replanned: N waves remaining (was M)`) (#614).
